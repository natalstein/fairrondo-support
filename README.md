FairRondo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

FairRondo helps coaches organize teams, lineups, substitutions, and playing time. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

FairRondo stores information you enter, including team names, player names, jersey numbers, position preferences, lineups, and game history, locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to servers operated by the developer.

App data may be included in device backups, depending on your Apple backup settings.

Camera and photo access

If you choose to import a roster from a photo or use the camera, FairRondo requests the necessary access. Images are processed on your device to identify roster information and are not sent to the developer.

Subscriptions and payments

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments through the App Store. FairRondo uses verified purchase information to determine whether your subscription is active. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Apple’s handling of payment and account information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Sharing information

If you choose to share a lineup, the information is sent to the people or services you select using your device’s sharing options. Their handling of that information is subject to their own privacy practices.

Advertising and tracking

FairRondo does not contain advertising or use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services.

Managing your information

You can edit or delete players and teams in the app’s Roster section and delete saved games in the Games section. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data; copies may remain in device backups.

Children’s information

Coaches may enter information about youth players. This information remains on the coach’s device unless the coach chooses to share it. Only enter and share player information you are authorized to use.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
